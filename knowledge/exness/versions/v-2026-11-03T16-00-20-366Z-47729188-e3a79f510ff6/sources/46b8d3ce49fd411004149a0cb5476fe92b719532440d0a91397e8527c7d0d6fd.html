Overview

The Exness Payments API lets you move funds between Exness accounts from your own systems: between your own accounts, or — if you are an authorized partner — from your Partner Reward Wallet to an account of another Exness client. You can also read the balance of your accounts and the status of your transfers.

Move funds between Exness accounts from your own systems.

Create an API key, confirm your setup with a read-only balance request, then create transfers and track them to their final status.

Start onboarding

Get started in 3 steps​

1
Create an API key

Create a key with the scope you need and store your credentials securely.

2
Send your first request

Read the balance of one of your accounts to confirm that your key, host, and request signing work.

3
Create your first transfer

Choose the guide for your use case — your own accounts or another client — then track the transfer to its final status.

Prerequisites​

  • •

    API key and signing credentials

    With the Internal transfers scope for transfers between your own accounts, or the Partner transfers scope for transfers to other clients. See Payments scopes.

  • •

    API host

    Provided during onboarding, together with your API credentials. All paths in this documentation are relative to that host.

  • •

    Request signing

    Implemented as described in Request signing.

  • •

    Account numbers

    The accounts you use. You find your wallets in your Personal Area under Payments & wallet, and your trading accounts under Trading → Accounts. Partners find their Reward Wallet number in the Partner Personal Area under Wallet accounts. See Accounts and identifiers.

  • •

    Supported accounts

    Only accounts on the Exness platform are supported. MT4 and MT5 accounts cannot be used as a source or a destination.

Expected result​

After the first request you receive the balance of your account with HTTP 200. This confirms that your API key is active, your request reaches the API host, your key has access to the account, and your request signature is correct. You are ready to create the transfers that the scopes of your key allow.

What you can build​

  • •

    Transfers between your own accounts

    Move funds between your wallets and trading accounts, for example to rebalance funds from an automated strategy.

  • •

    Partner payouts

    As an authorized partner, send funds from your Reward Wallet to your clients' accounts.

  • •

    Balance checks

    Check how much is available for transfers on an account before you send money.

  • •

    Transfer tracking

    Follow each transfer to its final status and reconcile the results in your own systems.

Core concepts preview​

Transfer
Transfers

A movement of funds between two Exness accounts, identified by transfer_id. Transfers cannot be cancelled or reversed through the API.

Destination
Destination type

Every transfer states whose account receives the money: own_account or another_client.

Lifecycle
Transfer status

processing while the transfer is in progress; done or rejected once it is final.

Safety
Idempotency key

A key you generate for each transfer; resending the same request with the same key never sends the money twice.

Accounts
Balance

The amount available for transfers from an account.
